Sword in Hand

The classic Bible sword drill. Swords ready… wait for the call!


How to Play

The call. Every round starts the same way: “Sword in hand! Find John chapter 4, verse 5.” Nobody moves until the call — Bibles closed, swords down. Then the countdown starts and the race is on.

  • Real Bible Mode (Practice): find the verse in your own Bible and slam FOUND IT! Your points stay locked until you prove the find — one try, and the verse stays hidden until after the check. Three ways to prove it:
  • Read It Aloud: read the verse out loud from your Bible — the app listens and checks it. The automatic check, and the strongest one.
  • Photo Check: snap a photo of the open page and a leader (or another player) confirms it's the verse. The app itself does not read the photo — a person checks it.
  • Quiz Proof: one quick question. For KJV it's about the exact words; for other versions it's about what the verse says.
  • Using NLT or another version? Pick it under “Which Bible are you using?” in Game Setup (tap “Change setup” on Home) — you'll be checked on the verse's key words and content, not KJV wording.
  • Hands-Free Voice (Real Bible Mode): turn it on in Game Setup and a round runs itself. Tap Start once so the app can use the microphone, wait for the call, then say “Found it!” when you find the verse — points lock at that moment. Reading starts on its own after a 5-second countdown; read enough of the verse and the round proves itself, then the next round starts automatically. Every tap button stays as a backup.
  • Hands-free works best solo or in a quiet room — other voices saying “found it” can trigger it. For loud group play, tap mode with a leader is better. Hands-free listening needs Chrome or Android Chrome; iPhone Safari can't listen, so the app falls back to taps there.
  • In-App Find Mode (Verified): tap the correct Book, then Chapter, then Verse. Wrong taps cost 5 points each, so accuracy counts.
  • Scoring: the faster you find it, the more of the 100 points you keep. Streaks earn bonuses. Run out of time and the round scores 0 — but the verse is always revealed so you still learn it.
  • Hints: Easy and Medium can show the testament and book family; Hard shows the testament only; Expert gets nothing. Using a hint costs 15 points.
  • Duel Room (multiplayer, up to 8 players): one player hosts a room and shares the 4-letter code; everyone else opens the game, taps Duel Room, and joins with it. The host picks the duel style and starts — the same verse is called on every phone at the same moment. First to find it buzzes in, then proves it (in an In-App Duel, finishing the Book › Chapter › Verse taps IS the buzz). A proven winner takes the round's points. Duel needs internet for the first connection — even in the same room — then the phones talk directly to each other. Works on GitHub Pages.

Profiles & badges: profiles live on this device only — no account, no sign-in. Earn XP for every point, climb from Seeker to Sword Master, and fill your Trophy Shelf (tap your profile at the top of Home). In a Duel Room only your name and avatar travel to other phones.

All verses are King James Version (KJV), word for word.
